## 1. Tge è `NALA-Understory-RS`?
**NALA-Understory-RS (`v0.5.0-nala.6.6.6`)** transfurma in ordinatur da datotecas Markdown (**Open Knowledge Format / `OKF v0.1`**) en ina **memoria d'agent auto-colliada**, che funcziuna a 100% independentamain sin macOS, Linux e Windows:
- **`RSI-001..004`**: Verificatur Evidence-Lock, SimHash-256 LSH (`O(N)`), Dream Gardener, chadaina Merkle a 256 bits e DLP da 5 zonas (`vault_primary`, `vault_enterprise`, `vault_partner`, `shared`, `public_pearl`).
- **`RSI-005..008`**: Sincronisaziun CRDT Multi-Noda (`node-alpha <-> node-beta <-> node-gamma`), compilatur GraphRAG da 2 sigls, Delta-Arena `< 18 µs` e secziuns criptadas `# [VAULT:zone]`.
- **`RSI-009..012`**: Cache N-Gram speculativa (`--ngram-draft`), harvester static Git-Diff (`--git-harvest`), Time-Travel Merkle (`--time-travel`) ed export DemoPod Zero-PII (`--demopod-export`).
- **`RSI-013..016`**: Snapshot binari `.okf.idx` cun MAC SHA-256 (`--binary-index`, `< 4.5 µs`), lease multi-agent per secziuns H1 (`--section-lease`, `< 900 ns`), rait GraphRAG federada (`--federated-rag`, `< 185 µs`) ed attestatur da citaziuns (`--attest-citations`, `< 14 µs`).
